VideoSaver

Privacy Policy  ·  Effective date: 18 May 2025

👋 Overview

VideoSaver ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile application that lets you save videos from TikTok, Twitter/X, and Instagram to your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle, why we handle it, and what rights you have.

Short version: VideoSaver does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server we operate. All download history stays on your device only.

📦 Information We Collect

VideoSaver collects no personal information. The only data stored by the app lives entirely on your device:

Data Where stored Purpose Sent off-device?
Download history (filename, source URL, timestamp, local path, platform) On-device SharedPreferences Show your past downloads in the app No
Theme preference (light / dark / system) On-device SharedPreferences Remember your display choice No
Download quality preference (HD / SD) On-device SharedPreferences Remember your quality choice No
Downloaded video files Device storage (Downloads folder) Save the videos you request No

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

🔑 Permissions We Request

PermissionWhy it is needed
INTERNET Fetch the video download URL from the source platform
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android ≤ 9) Save the downloaded video file to your Downloads folder
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android ≤ 12) Display thumbnails for downloaded videos in the history list
POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) Show a notification when a download completes

No permission is used for tracking, advertising, or profiling.

🔗 Third-Party Services

To download a video, the app contacts third-party extraction services. Your URL is sent to these services solely to retrieve a direct download link. We do not control these services and encourage you to review their policies:

  • tikwm.com — TikTok video extraction
  • twitsave.com — Twitter/X video extraction
  • snapsave.app / fastdl.app — Instagram video extraction

The app also uses the following open-source libraries. They operate entirely on-device and do not send data to external servers:

  • Glide — image/video thumbnail loading and caching
  • OkHttp — HTTP networking
  • Jsoup — HTML parsing

📢 Advertising

A future version of VideoSaver will include advertisements served through Google AdMob (or a similar ad network). When that update is released, the following additional data may be collected by the ad SDK:

  • Advertising ID (AAID / IDFA) — a resettable device identifier
  • Approximate location (country / region level, derived from IP address)
  • Device model, OS version, and screen size
  • App interaction events (ad impressions, clicks)
  • IP address (used for fraud prevention and regional targeting)
You will be notified and asked for consent before any ad SDK is activated. This policy will be updated with full details at that time.

Google's privacy policy for AdMob can be found at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time via Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation on your Android device.

🗑️ Data Retention & Deletion

All data (download history, preferences) is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time:

  • Within the app: Settings → Clear All History
  • From your device: Settings → Apps → VideoSaver → Clear Data
  • Uninstalling the app removes all app data permanently.

Downloaded video files remain in your Downloads folder until you delete them manually or through the app's delete option.

👶 Children's Privacy

VideoSaver is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises privacy concerns, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

🔒 Security

Because VideoSaver stores no data on external servers, the attack surface for your personal information is limited to your own device. We recommend keeping your device up to date and using a screen lock for general device security.

Continued use of the app after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
